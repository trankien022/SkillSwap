# Homepage: an online learning campus

## Brief and inventory

Audience: university students choosing an online skill session. Keep the existing
system sans, slate/emerald tokens, pill actions, manual verification, and real
routes. SkillSwap-specific material: student-to-student teaching, skill evidence,
expert review, document verification, credit wallet, 1 credit = 1,000 VND,
90/10 settlement, 30–180 minute sessions, 24-hour advance booking, online rooms.

Argument: learning with a peer is approachable (large learning illustration);
choose a skill to explore (real discovery links, no invented classes); joining
is understandable (three stages); trust and money have explicit rules (five
existing assurance facts); both learners and teachers have a next step.

Taste draw: borrowed-category, seed 421806237. Borrow a campus coursebook:
SkillSwap's two-sided exchange becomes two facing learning/teaching pages.
Keep brand type/palette. Override the perfume-counter hero and giant cropped
words because they contradict PRODUCT.md's accessible, clear product register.
Use a readable split hero and illustrated subject index. Generated scenes are
illustrative, never presented as actual members. No fabricated social proof.
The live interaction is an expandable learning journey and real skill links.
